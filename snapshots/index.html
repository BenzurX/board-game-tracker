v0.24 August 27, 2026
Golf games crown the right winner, and negatives can be typed on a phone
  • A custom game set to "Lowest wins (golf)" now actually crowns the lowest total - the scoring direction was being read from the wrong button and silently fell back to highest-wins every time
  • Reaching the target only ends the game; the winner is then whichever total is best for the chosen direction, so in golf the player who crossed it usually loses
  • A ± button beside each score field flips the sign of what you type - mobile keypads have no minus key, so this is how a negative score gets entered on a phone
  • The toggle appears in both the Enter Score modal and the tap-a-cell inline editor, wherever negative scores are legal

v0.23 MILESTONE August 17, 2026
Multiplayer that survives a locked phone, and a board that gets out of the way
  • A room now holds your seat through a refresh, a screen lock or a throttled background tab: a five-minute presence grace window in the Worker, a ping/pong keepalive with silent backoff reconnects, and a blocking Reconnect dialog only once the retries genuinely run out
  • Play follows itself - a new round scrolls every device to the live row, a turn change centres that player's column, and a shimmering your-turn card announces the handoff on the device that has to act
  • Score entry is gated on whose turn it is, New Game inside a live room restarts in place on the same code and roster, and Farkle's final lap is now measured in turn order rather than column order
  • The title, room-code and action bars now collapse together three seconds after you stop scrolling and return on any real touch, wheel or key - the app's own auto-scrolls no longer flash them back at you, and a floating ⊕ button covers the gap
  • Board animations carry information rather than decoration: totals count up, new rounds slide in, changed cells tint once, all of it inert under reduced-motion

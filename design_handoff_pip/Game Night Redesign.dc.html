YOUR DRAWING, FIVE WAYS
The idea in your version that the earlier rounds missed: the jade dot is not just the dot on the i — it is the centre pip of a five-face die, and the four ghost pips around it are the rest of that face. Every version below keeps that registration exactly (plate centred on the dot, corner pips on the diagonals) and changes one thing at a time.

Layout: standard 3×3 die placement — corners first, centre only on odd counts. Same geometry as the logo's face, 18px padding, 6px gutters, pip = 1/3 of the face minus gutter.

Same mark logic as 1A: cream squircle die, 11° tilt, two of four pips swapped to jade and bubblegum. Here the die stops standing next to the word and becomes the dot of the i.

CONSTRUCTION
Grid: 64 × 64 units. Every value is a whole unit or a half, so the mark redraws exactly at any size.
Ratios: plate radius = 16u = 1/4 of the canvas · die = 36u = 9/16 of the canvas · die radius = 10u = 5/18 of the die · pip r = 4u = 1/9 of the die · pip centres inset 9u = 1/4 of the die from its edge.
Tilt: −11° about the canvas centre (32,32). Never the plate, only the die.

Wordmark: Lilita One. P −3°, i +1°, p +3°. Die width = 0.47 × cap height, centred on the i stem, 0.06 × cap height of clearance. The font's own tittle sits under the die and is fully covered — no dotless-i substitution needed.

1A
STAGE 6 — PLUM & PAPER
Same emotional note as Stage 5, re-mixed. Eight accents, one spec, two grounds, ambient background instead of confetti.
Palette reasoning. The plum ground moved off blue-violet toward a warmer mulberry (#21131F) so cream sits on it like ink on a matchbox, not like text on a screen. The accents are mixed on one axis at a time: all eight share a mid-to-high chroma and are spaced ~40–50° apart in hue, but their lightness deliberately varies (marigold L≈0.80, cornflower L≈0.62) — that lightness spread is what keeps four filled chips distinguishable in one table header, including for red–green colour blindness, where hue alone collapses. Coral replaces Stage 5's pink-red so it does not sit next to the bubblegum; the yellow is pulled toward marigold and the green split into pistachio and jade so the warm and cool halves each have a light and a deep member. Both modes now run the same six fills with the same ink text — one button set, nothing to re-learn between themes. The deep versions of those hues (L≈0.35, chroma held) became a 3px border instead of a second fill palette: it gives a bright chip a hard edge against cream paper, adds a sticker-like weight on plum, and keeps every filled chip's contrast promise a single fixed fact rather than a per-mode one.

3 · SHAPE & ELEVATION
Hard offset shadows, no blur. Press = the element lands in its own shadow. Try them.
Border: every accent-filled control carries a 3px border in the deep version of its own hue — the thickness the sticker outline used. Neutral controls take no accent border. The cream 3px outline is now reserved for the FAB-on-photo case only.
Radii: 999px actions · 20px cards & tiles · 24px sheets · 12px chips · 10px swatches. Never below 10px, never square.
Shadow: 0 5px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.55) dark · 0 5px 0 rgba(33,19,31,.22) cream. Tiles 6px, sheets 0 -6px. No blur anywhere in core UI.
Hover: filter:brightness(1.06) saturate(1.03), 120ms — the fill catches a little more light, nothing moves. One composited filter, identical on every accent, and it never fires on touch, so phones only ever see the press.
Press: translateY(5px) + shadow to 0, 90ms ease-out. Tap targets ≥ 44px, action bar buttons 52px.
Rotation allowed: game tiles (±1.5°), FAB, invite/sticker buttons, mascot, wordmark glyphs, winner card, empty-state art.
Rotation forbidden: anything in a row of numbers a person compares — the score table, its header chips, totals, standings rows, numeric keypads, input fields. Baselines stay level, always.
4 · AMBIENT BACKGROUND
Three blobs, real opacity. This is exactly what ships — look closely, it should be barely there.
CONTENT SITS ABOVE
Shapes: three radial-gradient ellipses, 44–52px blur, sized 70–78vw so no edge is legible. Jade / bubblegum / marigold in dark; the deep light-mode trio on paper at half those opacities.
Opacity: .14 / .11 / .09 dark, .07 / .06 / .05 cream, plus a 3px dot grain at .045 (.03 cream). Total ambient contrast against ground stays under 1.15:1.
Motion: 46s / 62s / 120s loops, translate3d + scale only — compositor-only, no layout, no paint, no per-frame JS. Blur is applied once to a static layer; the animation transforms the already-rasterised layer. Three composited quads at ~7% of a mid-range Android's frame budget.
Layering: a single position:fixed; inset:0; z-index:0; pointer-events:none host behind an app shell at z-index 1. Nothing ambient can ever be above or catch a tap.
Reduced motion: animation:none — blobs freeze at their start position and remain as a static tint. Nothing appears or disappears.

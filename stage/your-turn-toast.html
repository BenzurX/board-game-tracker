"It's your turn" toast - 4 ideas

Fires on the device of whichever player (host or guest) just became active. Each frame replays on click; theme buttons show every palette. The player accent colour drives the shimmer tint, so the toast reads as "you" at a glance.

Theme Mode Player

A - Accent-bar toast, diagonal sweep

The existing bottom toast, unchanged in shape, plus a player-coloured left bar and a light sweep across the surface. Cheapest to ship and least disruptive; also the easiest to miss if the phone is face-down on the table.

B - Top banner pill, shimmer over accent fill

Solid accent pill dropping from the top, same slot as the update toast. Brightest of the four and hard to miss mid-game, but it sits over the header and can collide with the existing update-available toast.

C - Centre hero card, shimmer through the text

Full-attention moment: card pops in the middle, shimmer runs through the letterforms themselves. Most celebratory and best for across-the-table glances, but it covers the score grid, so it needs a short auto-dismiss (~2s) and tap-to-clear.

D - Quiet toast, shimmer in a travelling border

Plain toast with a light travelling around its border and a pulsing player dot. Subtlest option, safest for long sessions where a bright flash every turn gets annoying, but the shimmer is easy to miss in bright light.

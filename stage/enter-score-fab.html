Floating Enter Score button - 3 left-side treatments

Each frame is a live tracker screen with the shipped chrome auto-hide. Scroll a board: the title and room bars collapse once you leave the top, the action bar collapses once you leave the bottom, and the floating button fades in exactly when the action bar is gone. Scroll to the very bottom and the button hands the job back to the real bar. The two never overlap, because the button's visibility is the CSS inverse of the bar's.

All three sit on the left over the round-number gutter, so nothing they cover is a score. Their height off the bottom is measured, not hardcoded: the sticky Total row plus the active round row (tinted here) plus a 10px gap, so both stay clear whatever the row height turns out to be.
